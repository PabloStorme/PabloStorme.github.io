Approach

I took the long way into this. Two years of biochemistry in Antwerp and Ghent before admitting the obvious: I wanted to build things, not pipette them. I finished Applied Computer Science at HoGent and have been shipping software since.

I like the unglamorous parts — the pipeline that stops failing silently, the trace that finally explains what went wrong, the internal tool that gives a team back an afternoon. Outside work I build with friends, which is how Surpy went from an argument about a board game to something we all use.
